02 / WORKING NOTES

Notes

A few things I learned while making the calculator.

05 NOV 2026 · CALCULATOR DESIGN

Show the parts behind the estimate

A salary estimate is easier to review when it shows more than one final number. The calculator separates gross pay, deductions and estimated in-hand pay, with the individual components available to inspect. It is a reference calculation, not an official payroll statement.

05 NOV 2026 · KEEP IT SIMPLE

Make a small tool easy to open

The calculator lives in a single index.html file with its styles and scripts alongside the page. That keeps the project easy to open in a browser and straightforward to inspect without a separate build setup.
